SayItSo

Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

QUIETCOVE Inc. ("we") makes the SayItSo app (known as 미래낭독 in Korea). We built it so your words and your voice stay with you. You can use SayItSo without an account, and your voice recordings never leave your phone.

1. What stays on your phone

The affirmations you write, your voice recordings, your streak, your garden and your settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

2. Optional account and backup

If you choose to create an account, we process:

  • Account details: your email address, and your name as shown by Google if you sign in with Google. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Backup: the text of your affirmations and sets, your streak and garden progress, your settings and your trial start date, so you can restore them on another phone. Voice recordings are not backed up.

We keep this data until you delete your account. When you delete it, your account and backup are deleted right away.

3. Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never receive your card or bank details. The app checks with the store whether Premium is active on your store account. Refunds follow the store's rules: on Google Play you can request one within 48 hours of purchase; after that, contact us and we will refund duplicate charges or purchases that did not unlock Premium.

4. Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report through Google Firebase Crashlytics: what went wrong, the app version, device model, operating system and a random installation ID. It does not include your affirmations, recordings or email. Reports are kept for up to 90 days. You can turn this off anytime in Settings › Send crash reports.

5. Voices and other connections

  • SayItSo voices and phone voices are created on your phone. Your text is not sent anywhere to be read aloud.
  • Extra SayItSo voices are downloaded from our website, and when the app opens it checks a version file there. No personal information is sent; like any website, our host may log technical data such as IP address.

6. Permissions

  • Microphone (optional): used only when you record an affirmation in your own voice.
  • Notifications (optional): used for the daily reminder you set, and one notice before your free trial ends.

If you don't allow them, everything else still works.

7. Service providers and international transfer

We don't sell or share your information. We use Google LLC (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Crashlytics) to run accounts, backups and crash reports. This data is stored on Google servers in the United States. If you don't want this, you can use SayItSo without an account and turn off crash reports.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings › Account › Delete account. If you can't use the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Access, correction or questions: email us and we'll reply within 10 days.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Backups are protected by access rules so each account can only read its own data.

10. Children

SayItSo is meant for people aged 13 and over (14 and over in Korea).

11. Contact

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with its effective date and let you know in the app.
