SayItSo

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

QUIETCOVE Inc. ("we") makes the SayItSo app (known as 미래낭독 in Korea). We built it so your words and your voice stay with you.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, and we don't collect your personal information or send it to our servers.

2. What stays on your phone

The affirmations you write, your voice recordings, your streak and your reminder settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them too.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone (optional): used only when you record an affirmation in your own voice.
  • Notifications (optional): used only for the daily reminder at the time you choose.

If you don't allow them, everything else still works.

4. Connections and third parties

When the app opens, it checks a small version file on our website to tell you about required updates. No personal information is sent. The default voice uses the text-to-speech feature built into your phone. We don't share your information with third parties.

5. Children

SayItSo is meant for people aged 13 and over.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@quietcove.co.kr

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with its effective date.
