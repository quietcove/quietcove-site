Delete your account

SayItSo by QUIETCOVE Inc.

You can use SayItSo without an account. If you made one, here is how to delete it and what happens to your data.

In the app

  • Open SayItSo and go to Settings › Account.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

Without the app

Email support@quietcove.co.kr from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my SayItSo account". We'll delete it within 10 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in details).
  • Your backup: affirmation text, sets, streak and garden progress, settings and trial start date.

These are deleted right away and can't be recovered.

What is not deleted

  • On your phone: your affirmations and voice recordings stay on the device until you delete the app.
  • Purchases: Premium belongs to your Google Play or App Store account, not your SayItSo account, so deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the store.
  • Crash reports: anonymous crash reports aren't linked to your account and expire within 90 days.

See our privacy policy for details.
